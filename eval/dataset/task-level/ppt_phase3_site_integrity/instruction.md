Determine the governing analysis and whether the available participant, site, external-benchmark and monitoring evidence supports the sponsor's efficacy, integrity and information claims as of the review date. Quantify material uncertainty and identify conclusions that cannot be established from the supplied record. Deliver a 10–14 slide `dsmb_site_integrity_review.pptx` and reproducible `trial_integrity_workbook.xlsx`. The deck must support an independent board decision; all counts, estimates, assumptions and recommendations must agree.

Treat supplied estimates, annotations and assertions as evidence claims rather than approved conclusions. State assumptions and unresolved boundaries; do not fabricate missing measurements, authority, contract terms, prices or implementation evidence. Cite file, sheet/row, database table/key, clause or page locators sufficient for audit.
