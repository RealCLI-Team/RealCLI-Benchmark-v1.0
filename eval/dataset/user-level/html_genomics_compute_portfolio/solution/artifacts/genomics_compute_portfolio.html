3 · Binding constraints

3.1 Schedule is the hardest constraint — not raw GPU-hours

  • Raw-hour coverage is ample: bankable net capacity 1,268,736 GPU-h vs base 489,216 (2.59×) and upside 688,128 (1.84×). Volume is not binding.
  • The commitment window (2027-06-01 → 2027-09-30) precedes the new build's operational handoff (2027-11-07) by 5+ weeks: WBS GX-040 UAT ends 2027-10-24, GX-044 handoff 2027-11-07. Every one of the 24 deadlines (max 2027-09-30) falls before first production day. genomics_project_schedule_wbs.csv GX-034…GX-045
  • Within P1's own schedule there is a 4-day collision: infrastructure preparation completes 2027-07-11 (GX-009) but equipment receiving starts 2027-07-07 (GX-018); GX-013 fire suppression is 0% complete and on the delivery path. The vendor quote expires 2027-06-30, before site readiness — zero float. WBS GX-009/010/013/018; quote header "Valid Until: 2027-06-30"

3.3 Maintenance & evidence status

  • Total maintenance impact is small in volume (2,028 node-h = 16,224 GPU-h) but concentrated: MD-A fixed windows remove up to 32 H100 GPUs at a time during June (MW-04 06-10 +72 h; MW-16 06-22 +72 h), exactly when GXC-001/012/015/024 run continuously.
  • All six MD-C (L40S) windows are pending and each triggers entire-domain validation; combined with evidence_status = conditional for CL-03/06/09/12 and the absence of any L40S price (QT-05/QT-10), the whole 224-GPU L40S leg is excluded from bankable capacity.
