Methodology

  • Recomputed every disputed invoice from first principles per MSA clause 2: greater of (usage units x tier rate) and monthly commitment, plus disclosed migration charges, less credits, plus taxes/fees. All 18 recomputed totals equal billed totals to the cent.
  • Verified every applied usage rate against the signed Contract Rate Schedule rows effective for the account's contract version, tier and invoice period - all 18 applied rates appear in at least one effective signed row.
  • Scanned Invoice History for duplicate account/period billings - none exist.
  • Tested SLA claims against clause 3 mechanics, clause 1 scope rule and clause 4 evidence-agreement rule - eligibility indeterminate on supplied records (no region mapping, no threshold, monitoring coverage gaps/inconsistencies).
  • Cross-checked Customer Success preliminary notes against Dispute Records - the notes are rotated relative to the recorded assertions (e.g. CASE-002/008/014 notes assert regional outage while the disputes assert usage tier), so notes were given no evidentiary weight.
  • Screened every evidence artifact for collection method and chain of custody; artifacts with 'ticket owner only' custody, 'metadata absent' images, or label/method conflicts were treated as non-dispositive assertions.
  • Mapped each case to its resolution action (BRA-*) for owner, capacity, dependency (BRA-004/008/012/016) and mutual-exclusion (MX-1: BRA-001/007/013) constraints to drive prioritization.

Global assumptions & unresolved boundaries

  • All supplied estimates, annotations, manager notes and customer assertions are treated as evidence claims, not approved conclusions (MSA clause 4).
  • Invoice date is not recorded in any supplied source; the clause-4 30-day filing window is screened using the invoice due date as a proxy and flagged as indeterminate, never used as sole decision grounds.
  • Invoice History (156 rows) is treated as the complete billing population for duplicate screening.
  • The service_availability_timeline.csv (spanning 2027-01-01 to 2027-04-28) is the complete monitoring export supplied; it contains two internally inconsistent blocks for 2027-01.
  • The Contract Rate Schedule's overlapping effective rows are all treated as concurrently effective; no precedence rule is stated - recorded as an open contract ambiguity, immaterial to every disputed total because the commitment floor governs.
  • No migration amendment, SLA availability threshold, or account-to-region subscription mapping was supplied; determinations proceed without them and flag the gap as an evidence need.
  • Taxes/fees verified at a uniform 7.25% of net charges on all 18 disputed invoices.
